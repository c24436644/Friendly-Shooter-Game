# Friendly-Shooter-Game

Garden Zombies - GP Assingment

My initial idea for this game was to have you be a groundskeeper of a park. The park has been overrun by zombies, but you are an optimist, so you realize that dead zombies work as a really good fertilizer. You decide to continue your duty of looking after the park and so you kill as many zombies as you can to allow new life to grow and flourish throughout the park.

I thought it would be a good idea to create one level, because I felt that I would be able to make something pretty decent if I only had to focus on one scene. I also decide to make my game a top down shooter, again because it created a simple game that would allow me to focus on the quality of the game.

The first feature I began working on was the basic movement and layout of the level. I created the background and used some free assets I got online to create some cover for the player to evade the zombies. The idea was to break up the environment with bushes to add a bit of cover for both the player and enemy to use. This was to add a bit of challenge to the game.

Next I added the enemy movement. The enemy only follows the player once the player enters a radius around the enemy. I wanted the player to be able to escape the enemy and sneak past them so that they can get a better position to attack the zombies. This was to try and prevent the player from being overwhelmed by the enemies. I feel as though I did a pretty good job at this but sometimes it can still get a bit overwhelming with how many enemies can spawn if the player doesnt continuously kill them.

The next thing I began working on was the animation to show that the enemy is taking damage. It takes 3 hits for the enemy to die so I wanted the player to know that they are hitting the enemy. I created a simple animation that just changed the colour of the enemy for 0.3 seconds. I then added a laser sight to the players weapon to make it a little easier to aim.

Next I added a score system as to create some sort goal for the player. I decided to make the player kill 30 zombies to win. I thought 30 was a good number because it made the game challenging without being too tedious of a task.

Then I added the menus for the game. I created a start menu, a "How to play" menu, a game over menu and a victory menu. I made the "how to play" menu to let the player know that they have to kill 30 zombies to win.

Lastly I added the SFX and music. I chose light-hearted child like music to make the game feel less violent and to give it a bit more of a silly vibe. I feel as though this did help give the game a more light-hearted feel.

Improvements:

I feel like there is still a lot of room for improvement with my game. I think that the UI design could be better and the enemy scripts could be made a little cleaner. I also wanted to add a death animation to the enemy that would turn them into a flower to emphasise the fact that they are fertilizing the park, but I couldn't quite get it to work properly and it was taking up too much of my time for the rest of the assignment.
